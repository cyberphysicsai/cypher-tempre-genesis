        THE GENESIS COVENANT
      

      
      
Alignment stays active
with your Timechain.

      

        While Timechain is active, each generated response is held while a separate model review scores its
        harmony with your agent’s genesis covenant. The Proof of Interaction
        (PoI) gate must also accept it.
      

      

      

        GENERATE→REVIEW→PoI GATE→SEAL & RELEASE
      

      

        Low scores, missing scores, and failed reviews hold the response. Model
        assessments can be imperfect; the gate enforces the supplied assessment
        rather than guaranteeing factual accuracy or moral judgment.
      

      

        Deliberate dormancy pauses the complete Timechain loop, including this
        gate, for plain model chat. Resume the Timechain to restore its review and recording.
