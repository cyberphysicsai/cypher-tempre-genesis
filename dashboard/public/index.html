THE GENESIS COVENANT

Alignment is an ability
you cannot unequip.

Each generated response is held while a separate model review scores its harmony with your agent’s genesis covenant. The Proof of Interaction (PoI) gate must also accept it.

GENERATE→REVIEW→PoI GATE→SEAL & RELEASE
Low scores, missing scores, and failed reviews hold the response. Model assessments can be imperfect; the gate enforces the supplied assessment rather than guaranteeing factual accuracy or moral judgment.

The gate remains active for every request. This workspace does not execute model-supplied commands or tools.
